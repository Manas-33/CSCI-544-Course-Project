# Gate B is judged on T1 and T2

**Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Decided by:** the team, by merging this record · **Seen when decided:** the full pilot results (October 27) and the midterm feedback · **Post hoc**

## What happened

Gate B asked for an AUROC spread of at most 0.05 across T1–T3 (PLAN.md §7, before version 1.3). On the pilot:

- T1 and T2 agree within 0.05 for every verifier, in both domains.
- T3 (the inverted question) gives every verifier a lower pooled AUROC than T1. Most verifiers accept nearly every answer under it: Qwen 98.8%, Llama-3.1 97.4%, LLaDA 99.8%, Dream 98.6%. Gemma-2 (62.8%) and Llama-3.2-3B (46.4%) are the exceptions.
- Over T1–T3, no diffusion model passes gates A and B in the same domain, and Gemma-2 is the only AR verifier that passes both gates in both domains.
- Over T1–T2, LLaDA, Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Gemma-2-9B are eligible in math (`results/pilot/analysis/gates.md`).

## Decision

Gate B uses T1 and T2. T3 is a reported diagnostic and is not run on the full bank.

## What this means

- The primary result is math, with LLaDA as the diffusion verifier. The original rule's outcome ("repair") is reported beside it.
- That T3 measures how models handle a negated question, rather than unstable scoring, is our reading of the pilot and has not been tested.
- H1 is still tested only on new problems, with pairs and thresholds frozen on validation.
